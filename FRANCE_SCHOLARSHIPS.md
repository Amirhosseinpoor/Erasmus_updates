## 2. France Excellence Eiffel Scholarships (Campus France)

### The Full-Ride National Package:
* **Tuition Fees:** **100% Exempt/Waived** at public institutions by French law.
* **Monthly Living Allowance:** **€1,181 / month** (Master level).
* **Additional Benefits:** Free international round-trip airfare, national health insurance, and priority access to subsidized CROUS public student housing.
* **Golden Rule:** You can be presented by **ONLY ONE** French institution per year. Multiple nominations result in automatic rejection.

## Required Application Dossier (Checklist)

1. **Academic Transcripts & Degree Certificate:** Bachelor's records in English with grading scale.
2. **Proof of Academic Excellence:** Official class rank certificate (e.g., top 5% or 10%) if available.
3. **Curriculum Vitae (CV):** Highlighting research, projects, programming skills, and hardware/lab experience.
4. **Statement of Purpose (Cover Letter):** Tailored specifically to the master mention, laboratory, and research themes.
5. **Language Proficiency:** English certificate (IELTS 6.5+, TOEFL iBT 88+, or institutional Medium of Instruction certificate). No French required.
6. **Academic Reference Letters:** 2 recommendation letters from professors or research supervisors.
7. **Identity:** Valid passport copy.
