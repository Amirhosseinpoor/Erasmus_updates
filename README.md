## Detailed Program Information

### 1. [DeAI — Decentralized Artificial Intelligence](https://deai.ulb.be/home/students/admission/)
* **Field / Relation:** AI-related (accepts EE students as well, in addition to CS and CE)
* **Official Link:** [https://deai.ulb.be/home/students/admission/](https://deai.ulb.be/home/students/admission/)
* **Application Dates & Status:**
  * **Status:** Open *(opened 20 October 2026)*
  * **Deadline (Grant/Scholarship):** 11 January 2027 (12:00 GMT) *(strict)*
  * **Deadline (Reference Letters):** 18 January 2027
  * **Deadline (Self-funded):** 15 May 2027 (Non-EU) / 30 May 2027 (EU)
* **Countries:** Belgium, Spain, Austria, France, Italy
* **Universities & Current QS Rankings:**
  * **Université Libre de Bruxelles (ULB)** *(Belgium – Coordinator)*: **#=230**
  * **Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC BarcelonaTech)** *(Spain)*: **#371**
  * **Technische Universität Wien (TU Wien)** *(Austria)*: **#190**
  * **Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1** *(France)*: **#562**
  * **University of Padua** *(Italy)*: **#=236**
* **Program Timeline / Mobility:**
  * **Semester 1:** Université Libre de Bruxelles (Brussels, Belgium)
  * **Semester 2:** Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (Barcelona, Spain)
  * **Semester 3 (Specialization):** TU Wien (Austria) **OR** Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1 (France) **OR** University of Padua (Italy)
  * **Semester 4:** Master's Thesis at one of the 5 consortium universities

* **Field / Relation:** Autonomous vehicles, Control + EE
* **Official Link:** [https://master-epico.ec-nantes.fr/english-version/admission/deadlines](https://master-epico.ec-nantes.fr/english-version/admission/deadlines)
* **Application Dates & Status:**
  * **Status:** Open *(opened 1 November 2026)*
  * **Deadline (EMJM & Consortium Scholarships):** 31 January 2027 (23:59 GMT+1)
  * **Deadline (Self-funded):** 30 April 2027 (23:59 GMT+1)
* **Countries:** France, Germany, Italy, Romania, Mexico
* **Universities & Current QS Rankings:**
  * **École Centrale de Nantes** *(France – Coordinator)*: *QS Subject Mechanical: #102; unranked in global general list*
  * **Christian-Albrechts-Universität zu Kiel** *(Germany)*: **#611–620**
  * **University of L'Aquila** *(Italy)*: **#1001–1200**
  * **Politehnica University of Bucharest** *(Romania)*: **#1201–1400**
  * **CINVESTAV** *(Mexico)*: *Specialized research center affiliated with IPN (IPN QS: #741–750)*
* **Program Timeline / Mobility:**
  * **Semester 1:** École Centrale de Nantes (France)
  * **Semester 2:** In parallel at Kiel University (Germany), University of L'Aquila (Italy), Politehnica Bucharest (Romania), or CINVESTAV (Mexico)
  * **Semester 3:** Specialization at Centrale Nantes (France), Kiel University (Germany), or University of L'Aquila (Italy)
  * **Semester 4:** Master's Thesis under joint supervision
